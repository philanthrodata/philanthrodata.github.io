Have 30 minutes to talk about your data?

You don't need a pitch deck. Just tell me the one question you wish your data could answer.

What happens next

  1. I reply within two business daysTo set up a time, or with a question if I need more detail.
  2. A free 30-minute callWe talk through your question and the systems behind it, and agree on the one decision the work should support.
  3. A written scope and priceIf it's a fit, you get both in writing before any work starts. No obligation until then.
  4. 3 to 4 weeks to handoffA typical first engagement takes about 10 hours of my time. See services and pricing.

Small budget, or no budget for this at all? Say so in your message and ask about subsidized support.
