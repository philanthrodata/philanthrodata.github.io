Check a donor file

Export your donor or contact list from your CRM as a CSV, then drop it here. Works with Bloomerang, Little Green Light, DonorPerfect, Neon, Salesforce, and most other exports.

Add gift history (optional)

A second CSV with one row per gift: donor ID, gift date, and amount. This turns on year-over-year retention and LYBUNT/SYBUNT. Export at least three years of gifts.

Check the privacy promise yourself

  1. Open this page, then turn off Wi‑Fi.
  2. Load your file. The check still runs, because nothing is sent anywhere.
  3. Want proof? Open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, and load a file. You will see no requests.

Tip: you only need names, contact fields, and last gift date and amount. Remove notes and wealth data before exporting. A gift file only needs three columns: donor ID, date, and amount.

Column matching

How matching works. Records are grouped by shared email, phone, name plus ZIP, and street address. Common nicknames (Bob and Robert, Liz and Elizabeth) count as the same first name, and small typos are caught with a string-similarity score. People who share an address or email but have different first names are listed as households, not duplicates. Jr. and Sr. are never merged.

How retention is counted. Retention for a fiscal year is the share of the prior year's donors who gave again that year. Gifts on duplicate records are combined under the record we suggest keeping, so one person is never counted as two. Refunds and zero-dollar rows are ignored. LYBUNT means gave Last Year But Unfortunately Not This year; SYBUNT means gave Some Year But Unfortunately Not This year (or last).

Without a gift file, the recency section uses each donor's last gift date instead.
