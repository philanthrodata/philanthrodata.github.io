WEB & MARKETING ANALYTICS | COMPLETED JUNE 2026

Washington Improv Theater

$71K
in ticket sales that weren't being tracked
12 → 5
channels reduced to monthly action items
<6 hrs
of my time, start to handoff

The problem. WIT's website data was hard to trust. A misconfigured ticketing stream meant $71K in ticket sales weren't showing up in their analytics, 42% of visits were labeled "unknown source," and the monthly report covered 12 channels that nobody had time to read.

What changed. Once the ticketing stream was fixed, the numbers became something staff could act on. The 12-channel report became five monthly insights, including three clear actions:

  • Fix ticket-sale tracking, so revenue is counted where it happens.
  • Pause paid social ads and put that money toward search, where visitors were far more engaged.
  • Tag links in emails and posts, since most "unknown source" visits were really coming from them.

Where it stands. WIT's staff now pull those monthly insights on their own, with no ongoing outside help.
