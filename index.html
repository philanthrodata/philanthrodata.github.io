The engagement

How an engagement works

STEP 1: PREP

Goal alignment

We start with a short intake call to agree on one real decision your organization needs to make. That keeps the work focused on something useful instead of a vague goal like "understand our audience."

STEP 2: AUDIT & FOUNDATION

Fix, then build

We fix tracking problems first. Then we build reporting your team will actually use, sized for the time and people you have, not a data team you don't.

STEP 3: TRAINING & HANDOFF

Sustains without us

One staff member gets a standing AI prompt and a monthly checklist that take about 30 minutes a month. You won't need us, or any consultant, to keep it running.
