How a first engagement works

3 to 4 weeks, start to handoff

WEEK 1: PREP

Pick one real decision

A free call to agree on the decision the work should support, then a written scope and price. You give me access to the systems involved.

WEEKS 1 TO 3: AUDIT & BUILD

Fix, then build

I fix tracking and data problems first, then build the report or system around your decision, in tools you already use.

WEEKS 3 TO 4: HANDOFF

Runs without me

I train the staff member who will own it. They get a monthly checklist and a standing AI prompt that take about 30 minutes a month.

Pricing

What it costs

I charge on a sliding scale tied to your organization's annual budget, well below what this level of experience usually costs. A third of everything I bill goes toward subsidized support for small nonprofits that couldn't otherwise afford it.

ANNUAL BUDGET UNDER $500K
$100 / hour
ANNUAL BUDGET $500K TO $2M
$150 / hour
ANNUAL BUDGET OVER $2M
$200 / hour

Most first engagements take about 10 hours, so roughly $1,000, $1,500 or $2,000 depending on your budget. The first call is free, and you'll agree to a written scope and price before any work starts.

Small budget, or no budget for this at all? Ask about subsidized support. Tell me about your organization in the contact form and I'll let you know what's possible. The free data tools cost nothing to use.
Access and security

You stay in control of your accounts

The slowest step in most engagements is getting access to GA4, Google Ads or your CRM. You never share a password. You add me by email with a limited role, or create an address on your own domain for me, and you can remove me at any time. I never put your donor data into AI tools.
